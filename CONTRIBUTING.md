# Contributions

Go.Breeding is currently a proprietary, owner-controlled project. Suggestions and bug reports are welcome, but unsolicited code, writing, artwork, animation, audio, creature designs, or other copyrightable contributions are not being accepted at this stage.

Please do not submit a pull request or attach reusable project material unless SeirVed has first agreed in writing to contribution terms. Opening an issue does not transfer ownership and does not grant the project permission to incorporate submitted copyrightable material.

Before outside contributions are accepted, the project will adopt a written contributor agreement covering:

- the contributor's authority to submit the material;
- ownership or the license granted to SeirVed;
- permission to distribute the material in both free SFW and paid NSFW editions where applicable;
- permission to enforce rights in combined project releases;
- attribution, warranties, and third-party provenance.

This policy keeps the ownership chain clear for storefront publication and copyright enforcement.
