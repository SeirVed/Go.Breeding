> **Status: adopted.** The repository is proprietary and All Rights Reserved. The controlling terms are in the root LICENSE.md and COPYRIGHT.md files.

## Decision

All original Go.Breeding material remains Copyright © 2026 SeirVed, All Rights Reserved. The public repository is source-visible for transparent development; it is not open source and grants no general permission to produce or distribute builds, forks, ports, asset packs, mirrors, or commercial derivatives.

The decision applies to original project material from version 0.1.0 onward. Versions 0.1.0 through 0.2.4 were originally published without an open-source license and therefore already carried default copyright restrictions. The new notice confirms that status rather than retroactively granting rights.

## Why GPL and AGPL were rejected

GPL and AGPL are strong tools for keeping authorised forks open, but they intentionally permit copying, modification, redistribution, and commercial sale when their conditions are followed. That conflicts with the owner's requirement to remove unauthorised commercial copies and content-flipped editions merely because they copied and republished the project.

The future voting service therefore remains proprietary too unless SeirVed makes a separate written licensing decision.

## Distribution model

Both the free SFW edition and paid NSFW edition remain proprietary. Authorised players receive only the end-user permission supplied with an official build; ownership and redistribution rights remain with SeirVed.

One codebase will produce separate SFW and NSFW content packages. Paid adult assets must never ship inside the free package or enter the public repository. See distribution_model.md.

## Ownership hygiene

- Third-party components retain their own licenses and appear in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.
- Outside copyrightable contributions are not accepted until written contributor terms preserve a clear ownership or enforcement chain.
- Official releases should use annotated or signed tags, archived hashes, and copyright information inside the build.
- Store listings, release pages, and build metadata should identify SeirVed as the authorised publisher.
- A future legal entity or registered trade mark can replace or supplement the public SeirVed ownership notice.

## Practical limitation

Copyright deters and supports enforcement against copied expression; it cannot prevent piracy technologically, protect general game ideas, or automatically recover revenue. Storefront takedowns must be accurate and may be challenged by counter-notices. Material disputes should be referred to a qualified lawyer.
