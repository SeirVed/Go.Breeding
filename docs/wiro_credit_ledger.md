## Selection rationale

The first four characters are a deliberately non-redundant morphology set:

- **Cat:** ordinary fur, tail, muzzle, and familiar mammalian silhouette.
- **Phoenix:** feathers, wings, talons, and layered plume edges.
- **Void-born:** luminous/non-solid-looking surface and nonhuman hair/tendrils.
- **Naga:** humanlike upper body joined to a long serpentine lower body, testing a topology that cannot inherit a biped template unchanged.

The two motion finalists will be selected after the static derivatives are reviewed. This avoids spending motion credit on a source that failed the style or topology test.
