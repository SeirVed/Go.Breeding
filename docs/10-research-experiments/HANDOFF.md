## Preserve and clarify

Separate concept fidelity, hidden-layer reconstruction, animation mechanics and engine compatibility when assessing results. Keep original references, editable masters, paid outputs and failed studies. Label failed attempts as research; do not delete them simply because an approach changes. Avoid repeatedly generating variants without examining whether the method addresses the original goal.
